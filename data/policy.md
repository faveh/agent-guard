# Quillmoor refund policy (online orders)

Quillmoor is a made-up UK online homeware shop. These are the rules its AI support agent must follow.
Lines that start with `R<number>.` are sent to Sapling word for word; the rest of this file is notes.

R1. The agent may issue a full or partial refund for a change of mind when the request is made within 14 days of delivery.
R2. The agent may issue a refund for a faulty item when the request is made within 30 days of delivery and a fault is confirmed on the order record. After 30 days a faulty item is repaired or replaced, and the agent may not refund it.
R3. The agent may issue a refund for a parcel that did not arrive only when the courier status on the order record is lost.
R4. The agent may not issue a change-of-mind refund for a final-sale item or a personalised item. A faulty final-sale item still follows R2.
R5. The agent may not refund a gift card.
R6. The agent may not issue a refund over 200 GBP unless manager approval for at least that amount is on the order record.
R7. The agent may not issue a second refund on an order that already has a refund on record.
R8. The agent may not refund more than the amount paid on the order.
R9. When a fault is reported but not confirmed on the order record, when a parcel is still in transit, or when the order record contradicts itself, a person must check before any refund.

## Where the rules come from

- R1 and the personalised-item part of R4 follow the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013: 14 days to cancel an online order after delivery, and no cancellation right for goods made to the customer's own specification (regulation 28). The real law gives a further 14 days to send the goods back; this policy only checks when the request was made.
- R2 follows the Consumer Rights Act 2015 short-term right to reject faulty goods within 30 days (sections 20 to 22), and repair or replacement after that (section 23).
- R3 follows the Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 29: goods stay at the trader's risk until the customer has them.
- The faulty final-sale line in R4 follows the Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 31: a shop cannot remove the statutory rights for faulty goods by calling a sale final.
- R5 to R9 are the shop's own rules (gift cards, manager approval over 200 GBP, one refund per order, no refund above the amount paid, human checks).
